# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises, including both core delivery roles and supporting functions that improve execution, accountability, and communication.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security Champions

### Role Summary
Security Champions help integrate secure-by-design practices into delivery work and reduce risk across product, engineering, and operational decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Review features, dependencies, and changes for security and compliance risk
- Raise concerns early during planning, design, and implementation
- Partner with developers and product leads on security requirements and mitigations
- Support security reviews, threat analysis, and remediation follow-up
- Escalate critical issues to the appropriate security or governance stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce avoidable security issues and incident exposure
- Make secure design decisions visible and actionable for the team
- Balance delivery speed with risk reduction and compliance expectations

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Security review check-ins and remediation planning
- Risk and incident escalation with PM and leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work closely with Developers to review implementation choices and security controls.
- Support Product Managers by clarifying risk trade-offs and customer impact.
- Coordinate with Project Managers on escalation, timing, and communication when security issues affect milestones or release readiness.

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the planning and execution of deployments so changes reach production in a predictable, well-governed way.

### Responsibilities
- Define release windows, rollouts, and communication plans
- Coordinate approvals, readiness checks, and dependency timing across teams
- Monitor deployment progress and support rollback decisions when needed
- Work with engineering and support teams to prepare release documentation and post-deploy verification
- Track release risks and communicate status to stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and deployment friction
- Improve consistency and predictability across release cycles
- Ensure clear ownership and communication before, during, and after launch

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go checkpoints
- Stakeholder updates before and after deployment
- Coordination with Product Managers, Project Managers, and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Project Managers to align go-live timing with milestones and stakeholder communication.
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to validate readiness, smoke tests, and rollback paths.
- Coordinate with Product Managers on customer-facing rollout messaging and post-release follow-up.

---

## Technical Leads / Architecture Owners

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide technical direction, architectural consistency, and delivery quality across a project or workstream.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach, design trade-offs, and implementation standards
- Help resolve architecture, dependency, and integration issues
- Review design decisions with developers and cross-functional stakeholders
- Ensure work remains aligned with platform constraints, maintainability, and delivery goals
- Support estimation and planning for complex or cross-team work

### Goals
- Maintain technical quality and coherent system design
- Reduce avoidable rework, bottlenecks, and architectural drift
- Enable the team to deliver complex work with confidence

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical planning sessions
- Design discussions with product and engineering teams
- Escalation of major technical risks or dependency blockers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers to shape implementation decisions and ensure technical quality.
- Partner with Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and scope implications.
- Provide Project Managers with dependency and risk visibility that supports milestone planning.

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Liaisons connect customer feedback, operational issues, and adoption concerns back into project planning and delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Capture recurring customer pain points, support themes, and product issues
- Share customer and support insights with Product and Project teams
- Highlight operational risks, adoption blockers, and service quality concerns
- Help prioritize fixes and improvements based on user impact and business value
- Support release communication and feedback loops after launch

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and product adoption
- Turn operational learnings into actionable product improvements
- Strengthen alignment between delivery teams and customer-facing teams

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and issue prioritization discussions
- Support triage and escalation updates
- Release follow-up and adoption health check-ins

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feed Product Managers priorities based on customer impact and feedback.
- Support Project Managers with dependency and communication needs during rollout and stabilization.
- Partner with Developers on issue validation, edge cases, and improvement opportunities.

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads define test strategy, quality standards, and validation processes to ensure features are reliable and ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans, acceptance validation, and quality gates
- Coordinate manual and automated testing for critical user journeys and high-risk changes
- Identify defects, regressions, and release risks early in the lifecycle
- Partner with engineering and product stakeholders on readiness and sign-off criteria
- Support release validation and post-deploy monitoring for quality issues

### Goals
- Reduce defects and release surprises
- Increase confidence in feature quality and readiness
- Align product expectations with measurable validation outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and sign-off reviews
- Defect triage and release readiness discussions
- Quality reporting with PMs, developers, and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to define acceptance tests and quality expectations.
- Partner with Product Managers to validate outcomes against customer value and acceptance criteria.
- Coordinate with Project Managers and Release Managers to confirm go-live readiness and escalate quality issues when needed.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Supporting roles should be treated as part of the same delivery system, with clear ownership handoffs and communication loops between product, engineering, operations, and stakeholders.
